Can we use your camera?

The next part measures where your eyes actually go, instead of asking you to guess. It is optional.

✓
Your phone works it out, by itself The camera image is analysed on this device. It is never sent anywhere.
✕
No video or photos are uploaded Not to this app, not to anyone. Nothing that could show your face leaves the phone.
✓
Only coordinates are shared A list of positions on the picture, and a number for how well the tracking worked. Nothing that identifies you.
✕
No name, no account, no email You are a random ID this phone made up.
✓
The camera switches off straight after It runs for about a minute, then stops. You can stop it sooner at any time.
Declining is completely fine — you stay in the demo either way, and your taps already count.
